Three experiments to try

EXPERIMENT 01

Law of Large Numbers

Compare the running average after 10, 50, and 200 random observations. Does it generally move toward the population mean? Save a screenshot and explain why individual paths still fluctuate.

EXPERIMENT 02

Central Limit Theorem

Keep the number of repeated samples high. Compare samples of size 2 with much larger samples. How does the shape of the distribution of means change?

EXPERIMENT 03

Standard Error

Compare the spread of sample means for sample sizes n and 4n. Then choose the biased convenience sample: does increasing n eliminate selection bias?
